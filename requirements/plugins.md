# Requirements: plugins

Plugins: what a connection needs before it can work. A plugin is set up once and then used by every connection that depends on it. Connections are in connections.md.
Terms: the **API client** is the Google client the user creates once in Google Cloud; the **Google plugin** is the access to Google that a Google connection requires.
