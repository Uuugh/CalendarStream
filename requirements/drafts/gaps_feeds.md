## Coverage and limitations

13 owned rows: 12 New, 1 Covered. 12 distinct proposals FED-31–FED-42, after current FED-30. Repeated share, name and color claims reuse IDs.

Provider delays, naming and color limitations are explanatory guidance, not requirements imposed on Google or Apple. The stream-name proposal supplies the full claimed character set and applies it uniformly; it is proposed for Developer approval rather than inferred from the finite inventory samples. Native sharing and copying specify outcomes, not control placement or feedback mechanics. No application suites were run locally for this documentation-only change.
