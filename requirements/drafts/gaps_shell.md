## Coverage and limitations

10 owned rows: 8 New, 1 Superseded, 1 Not a requirement. 10 distinct proposals SHL-20–SHL-29, after current SHL-19.

Resource privacy means page rendering and its resource fetches; user-initiated external destinations such as maps remain owned by their features. This does not prohibit server-side source or Google requests. Full localization is a proposed product promise, not a claim that the sampled inventory proves every translation. Cross-browser section state remains a historical proposal: the Developer device-local decision specifically names view preferences, not section expansion. Shared folding expiry and per-phone controls remain recorded for common-UI reconciliation without edits to common.md. No local application suites were run for this documentation-only change.
