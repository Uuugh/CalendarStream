## Coverage and questions

26 first-owner gap rows; outcomes: 10 New, 7 Superseded, 5 Covered, 4 Not a requirement. 12 distinct new proposals CAL-66–CAL-77, after the highest existing CAL-65. Repeated print claims reuse proposal IDs.

The paper-fit claim needs an explicit readable overflow policy; the historical phone initial view needs confirmation against current responsive-zone decisions. Shared swipe, inline expanded-state and keyboard-control details are recorded in the outcomes without claiming UI-02 already covers them. Q-C1 was answered during triage on main a867490: defaults follow US versus other time zones, with no Automatic choice. That newer TIM-10 decision is used here. No fixed pixel-height rules, old key truncation rules or obsolete search/date limits are introduced. No application suites were run locally for this documentation-only work.
