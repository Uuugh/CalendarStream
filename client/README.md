# client

The Flutter client. `assets/` holds what the app ships with, copied from the retired app so the port keeps them:
`assets/images/logo/` (the five renditions the page used) and `assets/images/logo/source/` (the eight master files),
`assets/fonts/` (IBM Plex Sans and Mono, Material Symbols, with their licenses) and `assets/data/` (the emoji list and
its license). Declare them in `pubspec.yaml` when the client is created. Rules for Dart code: `packages/README.md`.
