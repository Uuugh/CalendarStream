How to get a client ID and secret (step by step)
  1. Open Google Cloud and sign in with your Google account: create a project. Name it CalendarStream and press Create. Make sure it's selected (top left) in the next steps.
  2. Turn on the Calendar API: open Google Calendar API and press Enable.
  3. Describe the app: open Google Auth Platform → Get started. App name CalendarStream, your e-mail as support e-mail → Next → Audience External → Next → your e-mail again → agree → Create.
  4. Allow calendar access: on Data access press Add or remove scopes. Tick openid and …/auth/userinfo.email, then under Manually add scopes paste:
    Press Add to table → Update → Save.
  5. Publish it so the sign-in lasts: on Audience press Publish app → Confirm. (In "Testing", Google signs you out every 7 days.) No review is needed for your own use.
  6. Make the client: on Clients press Create client. Application type Web application, name CalendarStream. Under Authorised redirect URIs press Add URI and paste exactly:
    Then press Create.
  7. Copy the two values Google shows (Client ID and Client secret) into the boxes above and press Save. CalendarStream checks them with Google straight away. Keep the secret private: don't send it to anyone or paste it into a chat.

How to turn on Calendar Sync in CCS+
  1. In CCS+, open Settings → Schedule Settings → Calendar syncing.
  2. Turn on calendar syncing and create a calendar password. Use a password just for Calendar Sync, not your CCS login.
  3. Press Copy link and paste it in the box above.
  4. On the server, put your UID in $MNT_DOCKER/.secrets/CCS_USER.txt and the Calendar Sync password in $MNT_DOCKER/.secrets/CCS_PASSWORD.txt, then restart the stack.
  5. Press Sync now. The checklist above turns green when CCS+ accepts the sign-in and the schedule downloads.

The password is only ever read from the secret file; it's never shown or stored on this page.
