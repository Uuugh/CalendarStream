Add to Google Calendar

Add to Google Calendar Or open in Apple Calendar / Outlook

Step by step

  1. Click Add to Google Calendar above. Sign in to the Google account you want it on if asked.
  2. Google asks "Add this calendar?" — click Add.
  3. The schedule appears under Other calendars on the left. Change its color or name there if you like.
  4. On your phone: it appears in the Google Calendar app automatically. If it doesn't, open the app's Settings, tap the calendar's name, and turn on Sync.

Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule, usually every few hours, so recent changes can take a while to show up. Always confirm against CCS before acting on a change.

  2. On a computer, go to calendar.google.com (this can't be done in the phone app).
  3. On the left, next to Other calendars, click + → From URL.
  4. Paste the link and click Add calendar.
